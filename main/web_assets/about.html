About E-Book Librarian

Welcome to the Digital Library

This is a community-driven, offline digital library and bulletin board running entirely on an ESP32 microcontroller.

You can read books directly from your browser, transfer them to a connected USB e-reader, listen to synchronized audio streams, and participate in the community board.
